Use the [demo cues](1.demo-cues-and-coverage.md) to prepare roles, records and files. Pause the recording between segments to change accounts or select files. Keep each demonstrated action and its result visible.

## 3:20 to 3:45 Security and ownership limits

We still need enterprise sign-in, CSRF protection, durable audit retention, backups, monitoring and real integrations. Automated review helps find defects; production ownership remains with your team.

## 3:45 to 4:20 Economics

**Screen:** On Business case, select Shared foundation works. Point to net benefit, payback, break-even ownership cost and capacity ceiling. Select the heavier-ownership and partial-avoidability presets; briefly adjust an input to show recalculation.

> Here the calculator tests assumptions. Removing two hundred fifty thousand annually, with one hundred thousand transition and one hundred thousand annual ownership, yields three hundred fifty thousand over three years. Heavier costs turn that negative. These are scenarios. Premium covers unlimited apps per licensed user, so ten more tools do not automatically mean ten more license bills.

## 4:20 to 4:52 Recommendation and next steps

**Screen:** Keep the calculator visible and finish on the recommendation or portal overview. Address the camera for the last sentence.

> First reconcile the invoice and renewal terms. Then pilot one real workflow with an accountable owner, measure total human effort, and have a second engineer maintain it. Migrate where the benefit survives those costs. Keep optimized Power Apps where it wins; consider Devin-authored code apps when Microsoft's retained controls justify continued licensing. Business users can request changes; engineers retain release responsibility.
