## Notes for the video and one-pager

- What the build demonstrates: one permission map, one mutation helper and one audit model reused by three thin tools; server-side denial of direct API calls; stale-write protection; linked counterparty→claim context; a registry for adding tools.
- What it does not show: Power Apps' maker experience for non-engineers, managed identity and governance, connectors, and environment and DLP policies. Those are the things the $250K actually buys and an owned build must replace or decide to drop.
- Honest framing: a working prototype in ≈15 minutes of agent time after the scope was fixed does not mean ten production tools cost ten times 15 minutes. Recurring ownership (review, incidents, controls, upgrades) dominates; see research §5 for the capacity threshold (≈0.7–0.8 engineer-years/year at $250K fully avoidable spend).
